\section{Interval estimates for the primary rates}\label{app:wilson}

Needle intact rates are exact fractions over small trial counts; Wilson
95\% intervals make the resolution explicit. Neighboring ratios at one
length are often not separated at these $n$; the separations the paper
leans on hold (tier-1 vs.\ the recent-window arm, 0 successes in every
cell; tier-2 recovery vs.\ its eviction arm), and the flatness of $1/32$
across lengths is a consistency observation, not a separation claim.
